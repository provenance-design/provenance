Neil's position: "The connection map needs to be a design itself... a designed item." The target is something with the same editorial conviction as the archive itself. Refik Anadol, Wind Map, Xenakis UPIC level ambition.

The Xenakis transduction of connection types into distinct spatial forces is the single most important design decision. If the types do not behave differently, the whole thing fails.

Reference material: docs/waves-model*.jpg (6529.io screenshots). Neil said these give ideas on sophistication but "I think we can do better."

The 12 Principles are applied reflexively in the design spec but are still WIP -- the phenomena they describe are real, the labels may shift.
