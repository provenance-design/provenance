## Voice/Quality Notes
- Descriptions: 3-4 sentences, specific, argued, curatorial voice — what makes design logic distinctive
- Significance: 2-3 sentences, take a position, sharp not generic
- Bad patterns to avoid: "demonstrates how...", "innovative approach to..."
- Good: Make design students think differently; positions that could be argued against
